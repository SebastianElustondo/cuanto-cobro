Privacy policy

A Quovra product · Last updated: August 2, 2026

Honest summary

This calculator does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, no forms, and we don't store what you calculate. The numbers you enter are processed in your browser and never travel to any server of ours.

What does happen technically

  • Exchange rates: the page queries DolarApi.com to get the current dollar rate. That request is made directly by your browser (DolarApi can see your IP, like any site you visit).
  • Analytics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying anyone.
  • Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts (your browser requests those files from Google).

Advertising

This page currently shows no advertising. If we add ads in the future (for example, Google AdSense), this policy will be updated beforehand, stating what data the ad provider uses and adding the applicable consent options.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any of your data by writing to contact@quovra.com. In Argentina, Law 25.326 on Personal Data Protection applies: the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP), the supervisory authority, handles complaints about non-compliance.

Controller and contact
